1. User roles

Customer (person who books a friend)
Companion/Friend (person who offers time)
Admin (moderation, payouts, disputes)

2. Auth & accounts

Signup/login with email + mobile, OTP verification
Forgot password via OTP
Google login (optional)
Accept Terms/Privacy at signup
Account deletion (required by Play Store/App Store and the DPDP Act)

3. Verification (most important for safety)

Age check (18+) at signup
Companion KYC: Aadhaar/PAN via DigiLocker or a provider like IDfy/Signzy
Selfie/liveness check that matches the profile photo
“Verified” badge on the profile
Admin approval before a companion goes live

4. Companion profiles

Photos (moderated), bio, languages, interests, city/area
Services offered (pick from categories), hourly rate
Availability calendar
Ratings and reviews, number of completed meets

5. Search & discovery

Search by city or area, with geolocation
Filters: service type, price range, gender, age range, language, rating, available date
Sort by rating, price, distance

6. Booking flow

Pick date, time slot, duration, service, and meeting place (public places only)
Booking request → companion accepts or rejects → customer pays
Booking statuses: pending / confirmed / completed / cancelled / disputed
Booking history for both sides

7. Payments

Razorpay/PayU/Cashfree checkout (UPI, cards)
Escrow model: hold the money and release it to the companion after the meet is completed
Platform commission, GST invoice generation
Cancellation and refund rules with time windows
Companion payout dashboard + bank/UPI payout (Razorpay Route or Cashfree Payouts)

8. Messaging

In-app chat, opened only after a booking is confirmed
Mask phone numbers and emails in chat, so users can’t take deals off the platform
Report or block from chat

9. Notifications

Email + SMS/WhatsApp + push for booking requests, confirmations, reminders and payments

10. Safety

Strict platonic-only policy in the Terms, with keyword filtering in chat
Report user, block user
SOS button during a meet that shares live location with an emergency contact
Check-in/check-out on meet start and end
Public-meeting-place rule

11. Reviews

Two-way ratings (customer rates companion and companion rates customer), only after a completed booking

12. Admin panel

Approve, suspend or ban users
KYC review queue
Bookings, payments, refunds and disputes
Reported content and chat review
Commission/pricing settings, service category management
Analytics: users, bookings, revenue by city

13. Legal pages

Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Safety guidelines, Child Safety, Community guidelines, Contact/Grievance Officer (required in India under the IT Rules)
